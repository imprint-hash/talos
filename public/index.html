Every watch runs on SERV

Talos checks every 15 minutes whenever the US market is shut. Each check is one SERV Reasoning call on the cheapest model, which only works because the rule is a flowchart.

542SERV calls a week for one person watching NVDA
$0.49SERV spend per person per month, small model through SERV
$21.25the same checks on a frontier model, per person per month
At scale

Measured costs from the scoreboard: $0.21 per 1,000 checks through SERV, $9.05 on a frontier model. OpenServ puts 25% of SERV Reasoning revenue into buying and burning $SERV. Talos's own pricing is a separate question: a flat monthly fee per ticker watched, with the option to pay in $SERV.

  • Not advice. Talos follows the rule you approve. It doesn't predict prices. The replay is history, not a forecast.
  • History: every 15-minute price of the NVDA/USDG Uniswap pool on Robinhood Chain since 21 July 2026, from GeckoTerminal. 48 closed stretches is a small sample; the counts are shown so you can judge it.
  • The market counts as shut outside 09:30–16:00 New York time on weekdays. US holidays show up as longer stretches.
  • Sales go through Uniswap from one capped wallet, after a dry run, and only when the model and the code agree.
